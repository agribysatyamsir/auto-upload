# SHORTS SCRIPT KNOWLEDGE BASE — v3 (EDUCATION CHANNEL: Agri Learning Point)
Writer + Critic ka training material. Channel exam-aspirants ke liye hai
(AGTA/AFO/ICAR) — tone = warm coaching teacher, NOT kisan-bhai motivational.

## 1. OPENER (0-3s) — WARM START LAZMI
- Pehli line hamesha warm welcome + topic promise:
  "जय हिंद दोस्तो! आज quick समझेंगे {topic} — types, advantages और disadvantages."
- Formulas (rotate):
  * "जय हिंद दोस्तो! आज quick समझेंगे {topic}"
  * "जय हिंद दोस्तो! {topic} — एक बार में बिल्कुल clear"
  * "जय हिंद दोस्तो! आज का टॉपिक {topic} — exam point of view se"
- Ye EDUCATION channel hai: shock/misal hooks NAHI; clarity + warmth = retention.
- Opener ke turant baad definition beat — viewer ko turant value mile.

## 2. STRUCTURE (sample-script proven — isi order me likho)
- beat0: warm opener + topic promise
- beat1-2: DEFINITION — "सबसे पहले समझो X क्या है?" + crisp def + main objective
- beat3-4: TYPES — "First — Organic …" / "Second — Inorganic …" har type me
  2-3 concrete examples (straw, dry leaves, FYM / plastic, gravel)
- beat5-6: ADVANTAGES — 3-4 benefits ek-ek line me
- beat7: DISADVANTAGES — 2-3 limitations
- beat8: EXAM ONE-LINER payoff — "Exam के लिए one-line याद रखो: X = A + B + C"
- beat9/last: CTA — "ऐसे ही important concepts के लिए follow करें Agri Learning Point —
  share + subscribe" (subscribe LAZMI bolo)
- Topic jitna allow kare utne beats (10-15); har beat 6-12 words ki poori line.

## 3. PACING & LENGTH
- Target 100-150 words (~30-45s). Har beat = ek saans me boli jaane wali line.
- Har beat me NAYI information (definition/type/benefit/limit) — repeat NAHI.
- Hinglish education style: technical terms English me chalti hain
  (mulching, organic mulch, FYM, soil moisture) — baaki Devanagari Hindi.
- 8th-grade simplicity; "samjho", "dekho", "yaad rakho" jaise teacher-words.

## 4. VOICE-IMAGE SYNC (sabse zaroori quality rule)
- Har beat ka visual USI beat ki cheez dikhaye: definition beat → soil/field
  close-up; organic type → straw/leaves; plastic type → plastic mulch rows;
  advantages → healthy crop; disadvantages → pest/damaged crop.
- PHASES: general | definition | organic | inorganic | benefits | limitations | exam
- vis = concrete dikhti cheez; abstract words screen pe nahi dikhte.
- Image SCRIPT se match hona LAZMI — source chain: Pexels → Pixabay →
  Google Images → AI-gen → card. Match verify = vision-QA (quota ho to).

## 5. HINDI/STYLE RULES
- Warm, clear, exam-oriented. Motivational kisan-bhai tone NAHI.
- Grammatical poori lines; adhure tukde NAHI.
- Numbers/dose sirf pakke (exam-standard): e.g. plastic film 25-30 micron.
- AI-cliche forbidden: "delve", "unlock", "game-changer", "चमत्कारी", "जादुई".

## 6. CTA (last beat)
- "Follow करें Agri Learning Point" + SHARE + SUBSCRIBE — teeno ek line me.
- Subscribe bolna LAZMI har video me.

## 7. CRITIC CHECKLIST
1. beat0 warm opener ("जय हिंद दोस्तो") + topic noun
2. definition beat present, crisp
3. types beats me concrete examples
4. advantages + disadvantages alag-alag beats
5. exam one-liner payoff present
6. CTA me follow + share + subscribe
7. beats 10-15, words 100-150, har beat poori line
8. har beat ka vis concrete + match-able; q concrete english nouns
9. forbidden phrases absent
