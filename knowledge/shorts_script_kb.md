# SHORTS SCRIPT KNOWLEDGE BASE (internet research se compiled)
Ye document Script-Writer aur Critic dono ka training material hai. Har rule niche
research sources (retention studies, creator playbooks) se aaya hai.

## 1. HOOK (0-3 second) — sabse high-leverage
- Pehli line = shock / galat-fehmi / nuksaan / contrarian claim. 6-9 words max.
- Proven formulas:
  * "Yeh ek galti poori fasal kha jati hai" (mistake/warning)
  * "90% kisan yahi bhul karte hain" (negative-curiosity)
  * "X ka sach koi nahi batata" (contrarian)
  * "Agar aap bhi X me nuksaan utha rahe ho, ruk jao" (direct-stop)
- KABHI nahi: welcome / namaskar / dosto / kisan bhai / "aaj hum janenge" /
  "kya aap jante hain" / did-you-know. Ye sab swipe karwa dete hain.
- Hook me topic ka core noun ho (fasal/cheez ka naam) — viewer turant jaane ye kis baare me hai.

## 2. STRUCTURE (Hook → Agitation → Points → Payoff → CTA)
- beat0: hook (promise of value or warning)
- beat1-2: agitation — galti ka dard/nuksaan ("kya bigadta hai")
- beat3..N-2: solution ke chhote concrete steps — har beat me SIRF EK baat
- beat N-2/ N-1: payoff — hook ka promise poora (natija: upaj/nuksaan ruka)
- last beat: CTA — SHARE + SUBSCRIBE, value se juda ("aisi jankari ke liye...")

## 3. PACING & LENGTH
- Ideal Shorts: 25-45 sec. Word budgets: 30s=70-110, 45s=110-160 words.
- Cut cadence: har 2-4 sec me naya visual. 30-40s video me 12-16 beats.
- Har beat = 6-10 words ki EK poori boli jaane wali line (ek saans me).
- Chhote vakya, "tum/aap" language, koi AI-cliche ("dosto", "namaskar") nahi.
- AVD target 65-80%; 3-sec view-vs-swipe target 70-80%.

## 4. VOICE-IMAGE SYNC (sabse zaroori quality rule)
- Har beat ka visual USI beat ke verb-phase se match hona chahiye.
- PHASES (kheti): sowing (bonai/beej) → growth (hari fasal/patti) →
  treatment (khaad/dawai/sinchai) → harvest (katai) → result (bhara godam/upaj).
- PHASE-LOGIC: script me jo phase boli gayi hai, visual wahi ho.
  * "beej bone ka time" bola → sowing visual (haath me beej, field me bonai)
  * "hari patti" boli → growth visual — harvest/paddy/carrot NAHI.
- Visual description (vis) = concrete dikhti cheez: field, haath, beej, paani,
  patti, bore, tractor, daana. Abstract (time, mistake, profit, stage) nahi —
  abstract cheez screen pe dikhti hi nahi.

## 5. HINDI STYLE
- Bolchal ki Devanagari Hindi; gaon me boli jaane wali bhasha.
- Grammatical poori line — adhura tukda ("fasal ghatati") KABHI nahi.
- Numbers/dose sirf tab jab 100% pakka ho.
- English jargon nahi; "urea" chalta hai (common word), "nitrogen level" nahi.

## 6. CTA (last 3-5 sec)
- Share + subscribe dono bolo, value se jodo:
  "Yeh video har kisan tak share karo, channel subscribe karo — agla video X pe."
- CTA short ho (6-12 words), warmth se, command se nahi.

## 7. CRITIC CHECKLIST (score & fix)
1. hook 6-9 words, shock, no greeting, topic-noun present
2. har beat 6-10 words, poori grammatical line
3. beats 12-15, total 110-150 words
4. phase-logic order sahi (sowing→growth→treatment→harvest→result)
5. har vis concrete + phase-match; q = concrete english nouns only
6. CTA me share+subscribe dono
7. forbidden phrases absent; koi angrezi jargon nahi
8. payoff hook ka promise close karta hai
